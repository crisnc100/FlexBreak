# AI Wellness Coach Beta Testing Guide

## For Beta Testers

Thank you for helping us test the new AI Wellness Coach feature! This guide will help you get started and provide valuable feedback.

### What is AI Wellness Coach?

AI Wellness Coach is a new feature that checks in with you about your physical wellness and provides personalized movement and stretch suggestions through notifications. Free users can chat on Wednesdays, while premium users get daily access.

### Getting Started

1. **Update the App**: Make sure you have the latest beta version
2. **Enable the Feature**: 
   - Go to Settings
   - Find "AI Flex Coach (beta)"
   - Toggle it on
   - Allow notifications when prompted
3. **First Check-in**:
   - You'll receive a notification at 2 PM
   - Tap and reply directly in the notification
   - The AI will ask for your first name
   - Then you can share how you're feeling

### What to Test

#### Daily Use
- Reply to the 2 PM check-in notification
- Share real wellness concerns (back pain, stress, fatigue, etc.)
- Try the suggested exercises
- Rate effectiveness after 30 minutes

#### Feature Testing
- **Free Users**: Try using on different days (should only work Wednesday)
- **Free Users**: Test the daily limit (1 chat per Wednesday)
- **Premium Users**: Test daily access
- **All Users**: Try different types of wellness issues

#### Edge Cases
- Use with poor internet connection
- Reply to notifications with app closed
- Try very long messages
- Test while in Do Not Disturb mode

### Feedback We Need

Please track and report:

1. **Response Quality**
   - Were suggestions helpful?
   - Were they appropriate for your situation?
   - Did they fit your work environment?

2. **Technical Issues**
   - Any crashes or errors?
   - Notification problems?
   - Slow responses?

3. **User Experience**
   - Is the 2 PM time good?
   - Is once per week enough for free users?
   - How's the notification interaction?

4. **Effectiveness**
   - Did suggested exercises help?
   - Which worked best?
   - What didn't work?
