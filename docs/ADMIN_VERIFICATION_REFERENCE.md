## 🎯 **What to Look For:**

### **APPROVE Students if they provide:**
- ✅ School/university name
- ✅ Student email (.edu address is great)
- ✅ Student ID number
- ✅ Enrollment details
- ✅ Clear indication they're currently enrolled

### **APPROVE Office Workers if they provide:**
- ✅ Company name
- ✅ Work email (business domain preferred)
- ✅ Job title/position
- ✅ Office/hybrid work arrangement
- ✅ LinkedIn profile (optional but helpful)

### **REJECT if:**
- ❌ Obviously fake information
- ❌ Remote worker trying to get office discount
- ❌ No verification details provided
- ❌ Graduated student trying to use old status
