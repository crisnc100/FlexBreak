# How AI Wellness Works - Simple Guide

## The Notification
When you receive an AI Wellness notification, it looks like this:

```
🤖 AI Wellness Check
Recent: 😊→😐→😓
Tap to chat or use voice 🎙️
```

## Your Options:

### 1. Just Tap the Notification
- Opens the app
- Shows a floating bubble (not a full screen)
- You can type or use voice
- Bubble can be dragged around or minimized

### 2. Swipe Down / Long Press
Shows 2 action buttons:
- **💬 Type Reply** - Opens a text field IN the notification (no app needed)
- **🎙️ Voice Reply** - Opens the app with voice mode ready

## The Wellness Bubble

When the app opens, you'll see:
- Small floating bubble with 🤖
- Tap to expand
- Type your message or tap the microphone
- AI responds in the same bubble
- Drag it anywhere on screen
- Continue using the app normally

## Voice Mode

When you tap 🎙️ Voice Reply:
1. App opens with bubble expanded
2. Voice indicator shows "Voice mode ready"
3. Speak your response
4. AI processes and responds

## What Makes This Unique

- **No forced choices** - Say whatever you want
- **Voice support** - Talk instead of type
- **Non-intrusive** - Floating bubble, not full screen
- **Your mood history** - Shows your journey over time
- **Works everywhere** - All iOS/Android versions

## Testing

In Settings > AI Wellness:
1. **Test Notification Now (5s)** - Sends a test notification
2. **View Schedule** - See when notifications are scheduled

That's it! Simple, flexible, and focused on letting you express yourself naturally.
